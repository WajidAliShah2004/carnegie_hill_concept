Eight design concepts for nyframing.com

Each concept shows a home page and a gallery page. All eight use the shop's real photographs, the real logo, the reviews already on the current site, and the address, hours, and phone number as the current site lists them. Those details still need your confirmation. Pick one, or tell us which parts of two to combine.

The newer four

No colour in these four was picked by eye. Every value is the median pixel of a named region of one of the shop's own photographs, recorded in the sourcing notes.

Concept 1

Mat board

Quiet and gallery like. Every picture is mounted on a board with a deeper margin at the bottom and its label printed in that margin, which is how a mat is actually cut. Cool grey greens read off the mat board in the shop's own photographs, with one olive gilt hairline.
Concept 2

The hang

The shop's frames are black and its mats are white, so the page carries no colour of its own and the work supplies all of it. Pictures sit in rows that share one centre line, the way they are hung on a wall, and the line runs the length of the row behind them.
Concept 3

Heritage craft

The older, quieter shop. Caslon on a dark wall the colour of the shop's own window frames, with the gilt taken off a gilt frame in the gallery, so it is olive rather than bright yellow. The gallery pieces hang on the dark wall.
Concept 4

Shop front

Built from the storefront. The burgundy awning crowns the page with its scalloped edge, the shop itself runs full width underneath, and the hours sit on a black sign strip below that. The yellow of the facade appears as one band, not as a whole page.

The first four

The opening set, kept here so you can compare. Heritage craft and Shop front were later rebuilt, and both versions of each are on this page. Colours in this set were chosen by eye rather than measured.

Concept 5

Heritage craft

Gilt and black. One ornate gold framed piece hangs on a dark wall beside a Caslon headline. Sections alternate between the dark wall and a linen mat. The quiet, older shop feel.
Concept 6

Shop front

Built from the actual storefront: the burgundy awning becomes the masthead, the yellow facade becomes the hero, and hours sit on a black sign strip underneath. Flat colour blocks, thick black rules, no cards or shadows.
Concept 7

Familiar evolution

The current site, tidied. Same logo, same headline, same wall photo, same page order. Contact details move from the footer to just under the hero, and the reviews are the ones already on the site.
Concept 8

Gallery-led

The gallery is the page. A wall of real framed pieces opens the site, the headline sits on a white plate like a wall label, and every photo gets a small museum style caption. Condensed type, black and white, one gilt rule.
