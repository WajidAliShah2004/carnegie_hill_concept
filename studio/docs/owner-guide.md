# Putting photos on the website

This is everything you need. It works the same on a phone, a tablet, or a computer.

Your link: `https://<name>.sanity.studio/owner`

Save it to your home screen so it is one tap away.

> [Screenshot goes here: the home screen icon]

## Add a photo

1. Open the link and sign in.
2. Tap the blue **Add photo** button at the bottom.
3. Tap the photo box. Your phone asks whether to take a photo or choose one you
   already have. Pick one.
4. Wait for the picture to finish loading.
5. Tap one of the four buttons to say what kind of photo it is.
6. Tap **Publish** at the bottom.

The website updates by itself a few minutes later.

> [Screenshot goes here: the four type buttons]

## Add a caption, if you want one

The caption box sits under the four buttons. You do not have to use it.

1. Type what is in the picture, in your own words.
2. Tap **Publish**.

Only write what you can see in the photo. People who cannot see the picture have
this read out to them, so it is worth a few plain words.

## Put a photo on the front page slider

1. Open the photo, or add it as above.
2. Turn on **Show on the front page slider**.
3. Tap **Publish**.

Turn the same switch off to take it out of the slider. The photo stays in the
gallery.

> [Screenshot goes here: the slider switch]

## Take a photo off the website

1. Open the photo.
2. Tap the three dots next to **Publish**.
3. Tap **Unpublish**.

The photo comes off the website but stays in your list, so you can put it back
later. To remove it for good, tap the three dots and then **Delete**.

> [Screenshot goes here: the three dots menu]

## Change the order

1. Go back to the list of photos.
2. Press and hold the handle on the left of a photo.
3. Drag it up or down and let go.

The order you set here is the order on the website. You do not need to publish
anything after moving photos.

> [Screenshot goes here: dragging a photo up the list]

## If something goes wrong

- The picture will not load. Check the phone signal and try again.
- **Publish** is grey. Something is missing. Look for the red message on the form.
  It is nearly always the four buttons, so tap one.
- A photo is not on the website yet. Give it five minutes, then reload the page.

If it still will not work, call or message Juko.
